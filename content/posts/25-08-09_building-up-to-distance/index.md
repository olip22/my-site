## Intro

After the short pub ride the other week, I wanted to push a little further. Nothing huge — just something long enough to feel like progress. We planned a ~25 km loop with a picnic stop in the middle, a simple way to test how I’d handle more time in the saddle.

## The ride and picnic

It was a lovely sunny day, and I felt excited at the start. My knees did ache here and there, but not badly enough to stop me riding or leave me sore the next day. 

![Alt text](25-08-09_picnic-ride-01.webp)

The picnic halfway was a highlight — a proper reset before rolling on again. The second half flew by once we’d eaten.

## Fitness and feeling

Overall, the ride felt longer than I expected but still manageable. The flat stretches were fine, but the hills were tough — they drained my legs quickly and showed me how far I’ve got to go. Still, finishing this ride gave me a confidence boost that longer distances are possible.

## Gear thoughts

I’m definitely underprepared on gear. Everything just went into a rucksack, and I don’t even have a bottle holder yet. Not ideal. It worked for now, but it’s clear I need a few basics before I think about going further.

## Conclusion and next goal

By the end of the ride we were shattered and slept hard that evening. But it felt like a step in the right direction. My knees held up, the distance didn’t break me, and I enjoyed it. Next up: pushing towards something closer to 30 miles and seeing how I cope.
